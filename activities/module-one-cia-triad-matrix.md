# Module One Activity: CIA Triad Control Matrix
**CYB-200 | Cybersecurity Foundations**

## Objective
Map NIST security controls to the appropriate CIA Triad pillar (Confidentiality, Integrity, or Availability) and justify each selection with peer-reviewed sources.

---

## CIA Triad Control Mapping

| Control | CIA Category | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| **IA — Identification & Authentication** | Confidentiality | Without strong authentication, unauthorized users can access systems and compromise the confidentiality of data within them. |
| **SI — System & Information Integrity** | Integrity | Unauthorized modifications to data can go undetected, compromising accuracy and reliability. Integrity controls ensure data remains trustworthy. |
| **CP — Contingency Planning** | Availability | Without contingency planning, systems and data may become unavailable during disruptive events, directly impacting business continuity. |

---

## Key Concepts
- **Confidentiality** — Ensuring data is accessible only to authorized individuals
- **Integrity** — Ensuring data is accurate, complete, and unaltered
- **Availability** — Ensuring systems and data are accessible when needed

The CIA Triad forms the foundational framework for evaluating all security controls and decisions in cybersecurity.
