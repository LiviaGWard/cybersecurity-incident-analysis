# Project Two: Incident Analysis Brief
**CYB-200 | Cybersecurity Foundations | Grade: A**

## Scenario Summary
A financial services firm handling high-income accounts discovers that cleaning crew personnel have been observing analysts' screens and removing documents from "destroy" bins — exposing sensitive client financial data to unauthorized individuals.

---

## I. Scenario Analysis

### A. Primary Security Objective: Confidentiality
Confidentiality is the most critical objective here. Financial analysts are contractually obligated to protect client data. The cleaning crew's unauthorized access to screens and physical documents represents a direct breach — potentially enabling identity theft, financial fraud, and severe reputational damage to the firm.

### B. Negative Impacts Across People, Processes, and Technologies
| Area | Impact |
|---|---|
| **People** | Erosion of client trust; potential loss of business and damaged relationships |
| **Processes** | Internal investigations, mandatory retraining, and new data handling procedures divert resources from normal operations |
| **Technology** | Need for enhanced encryption, physical access controls, and monitoring systems — costly and time-intensive to implement |

---

## II. Recommendations

### A. Fundamental Security Design Principles Applied

**Separation of Duties:**
Divide responsibilities so no single individual or group has unmonitored access to sensitive materials. Cleaning staff should never be in secure areas without escort or supervision.

**Least Privilege:**
Access to sensitive areas and data should be restricted to only those with a defined, justified need. Cleaning crew schedules and access zones should be controlled and audited.

### B. Additional Controls Recommended
- Privacy screens on all analyst workstations
- Clean desk policy enforcement — no documents left unsecured
- Supervised access protocol for all non-analyst personnel in secure areas
- Security camera coverage of workspace areas
- Physical document destruction witnessed or logged
